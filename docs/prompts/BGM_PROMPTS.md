# Mojiworld — BGM Prompts (Suno)

Eight tracks for the eleven maps that currently fall back to the hub theme.
Suno, Custom mode, **Instrumental ON**. Paste the prompt, give it the title,
generate two takes and keep the better one. Aim for 2-3 minutes. Download as
WAV if you can, MP3 otherwise — hand it over raw, I'll do the rest.

### 1. Fungal Hollow / Wildflower Plains
A glowing mushroom grove and a field of magenta wildflowers. The first music
outside the village, so: curious and wide-eyed, never threatening.

> Whimsical storybook orchestral, curious and wandering. Pizzicato strings and
> marimba skipping along, clarinet and oboe trading a gentle melody, celesta
> and glockenspiel sparkling on top, soft horns underneath, light shaker
> keeping a walking pace. Luminous, unhurried, quietly magical.

### 2. Sunset Coast
A huge orange sun sitting on the water, purple sky, silhouetted trees.

> Warm nostalgic orchestral folk, golden hour by the sea. Fingerpicked nylon
> guitar carrying the theme, a solo flute answering it, slow lush strings
> swelling underneath, soft electric piano and harp. Reflective, tender,
> spacious, a little bittersweet.

### 3. Sky Garden
A meadow floating in an enormous blue sky full of white cloud towers.

> Soaring uplifting cinematic orchestral, open-sky adventure. Rising string
> ostinato, a gentle heroic horn melody, flutes shining above it, harp
> cascades, bells and celesta, swelling timpani. Bright, hopeful, weightless,
> always moving forward.

### 4. Storm Crest / Thunder Plateau
Violet storm clouds full of white lightning over a cold blue plateau. Charged
and dangerous, but exhilarating rather than evil.

> Electric orchestral tension, a highland under a lightning storm. Driving low
> strings, taiko and timpani on the downbeats, stabbing brass swells, a keening
> solo violin over the top, anvil and metallic percussion, a sparse synth
> arpeggio glinting like static. Dramatic and charged.

### 5. Sweet Candy Canyon
Pink and orange candy trees, gumdrop boulders, a turquoise syrup river.

> Playful bouncy orchestral, cartoon candyland. Bubbly marimba and xylophone
> melody, plucked staccato strings, a comic tuba and bassoon bassline,
> glockenspiel counter-melody, toy piano fills, woodblock and tambourine.
> Cheerful, silly, endlessly energetic.

### 6. The Bastion - Throne Room
A candlelit stone hall with crimson banners, a stained-glass arch and a throne
on a low dais. Intimate and lived-in, not a parade ground.

> Solemn noble chamber music, a candlelit stone throne hall. Soft church organ
> drone, a string quartet carrying a dignified slow processional, solo cello
> on the melody, harp at the end of each phrase, distant muted horn calls,
> faint timpani heartbeat. Reverent, warm, quiet.

### 7. Tidal Lagoon
Turquoise shallows between sandstone cliffs on a bright clear day.

> Calm bright aquatic music, a sunlit shallow lagoon. Rippling harp and
> celesta figures, warm vibraphone, soft legato strings, flute and recorder
> trading an easy pastoral melody, guitar harmonics, gentle shaker.
> Refreshing, weightless, peaceful.

### 8. Distorted Portal
A blood-red dusk over an abandoned pagoda and dead power lines. A place that
is wrong. Dread without violence.

> Eerie liminal dark ambient with eastern instruments, a red dusk that should
> not exist. Detuned koto and shamisen plucks, glassy bowed drones, low
> breathing shakuhachi phrases, sparse prepared piano with long decay,
> reversed cymbal swells, a slow deep tom like a heartbeat, a faint bell
> drifting out of tune. Unsettling, hollow, suspended.
