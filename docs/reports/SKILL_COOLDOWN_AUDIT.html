1. Bugs

findingevidencestatus
B1A portal hop took a quarter off War of Banners' cooldown, and Meteor Sigil was charged twice. When a map change or a death cuts an ultimate's window short, one repair function charges its cooldown. It used its own formula instead of the one the window's own close uses. Finishing the enrage leaves War of Banners on 59.8 s; walking through a portal inside it left 44.3 s. Meteor Sigil: the repair charged 36.9 s, then the old window ran out on the next map and charged it again (33.2 s, 7.7 s later). The repair was also still reading _sageUntil, a field nothing has written since the Sigil moved to game time. FIXED the repair charges exactly what each window's close charges. New test ult_window_repair_test.mjs: 3 of 6 fail on the old build, 6 of 6 pass.
B2Meteor (Archmage F) only lands on monsters touching the mage. v0.30.869 made every falling fireball hit only where the drawn rock is (per your request, for monsters and skills). The Meteor skill still drops 80 px ahead of the mage, so its 136 px blast no longer reaches a monster at casting range. One cast at a monster whose edge is 0-80 px from the mage: 283% of a basic. 120 px: 50%. 160 px or more: 0%. The skill table fell from 367% (v0.30.814) to 65%. Target for its tier: ~550%. YOUR CALL keep your hitbox and aim the meteor at the nearest monster ahead (as Meteor Sigil already does), or drop it further ahead.
B3The skill table undercounted every skill you press more than once. The measuring tool presses each skill once. War of Banners, Meteor Sigil, Elemental Apotheosis, Kage Rush and Voidrift Blink are several presses per cast. It also pins the random numbers, which drops all 22 Arrow Rain arrows on one spot past the dummy, so Arrow Rain has always read 0%. One press vs one full use: War of Banners 941% → 19,752%, Meteor Sigil 387% → 3,867%, Elemental Apotheosis 814% → 2,814%. The v0.30.772 tier pass and the Skill Editor both read these one-press rows, and the tier pass skips a 0% row like Arrow Rain as utility. TOOL FIXED skill_tabulation.mjs presses them as full uses and gives Arrow Rain a seeded spread. No game change.
B4Spellweaver goes past the cooldown cap. The mage tree node takes 1 s off every cooldown on any frame with a crit. All other cooldown reduction is capped at 55%; this has no cap. At a 40% crit rate, over 30 s: Hexmaster cast its 34 s ultimate 3 times (1 without it), damage +58%. Elementalist +58%, Necromancer +54%, Sage +25%, Archbishop +24%. YOUR CALL see recommendation 4.

Checked and sound

  • Cooldowns shown are cooldowns charged. All 70 skills with a cooldown were cast at ranks 0, 5 and 10. The cooldown each one really set matched the Skills panel every time, including charge skills (Voidrift Blink, Kage Rush, Elemental Apotheosis) and windows (Deadeye, Deadeye Protocol, Meteor Sigil).
  • Cooldowns stop while paused. A 5,000 ms cooldown still read 5,000 after 92 paused steps.
  • Every cast path waits for the cooldown. The held-key poll, the edge-triggered Z, the warrior charge release and the double-tap warp all check readiness before casting.
  • Summons do not stack past their cooldown. Rank adds 1 s per rank, so War Machine's turrets (45 s) and the Sky Hunter eagle (42 s) can outlive their 45 s cooldown. A recast replaces them rather than adding a second set, and the wolf pack is capped at 5.
  • No page errors while casting all 72 skills alone and into a six-monster cluster, nor across 42 rotations (21 kits × 30 s and × 90 s).
  • Recent changes read clean: the Endless Express removal (saves are purged of it, no live references), Felina's typed amount (validates, can't overdraw either side), the boss boon 1/2/3 keys (only while the card is open, never inside a text box).
  • Chain Lightning's 1-of-6 in the cluster table is the test layout, not the skill: the nearest monster is the one behind the mage, and the next one is outside the 300 px jump. In a line of six ahead of the mage it hit all six (2,504 down to 1,528, 8% less per jump).

3. Cooldowns

How a cooldown is worked out

Every cast waits its table cooldown × 0.75 (the global cut from v0.25.169), × 0.90 more for a mage, × Quick Draw for Arrow Shot, × the rank-5 / rank-10 perk, × Hallowed Field and rogue Momentum (these two capped together at 55%). Quickening can skip a cooldown outright (up to 30% of casts, never on the charge skills or Holy Shield). The Skills panel shows the result of that arithmetic, and it matched the game for every skill at every rank tested.

What does not fit

  • Master damage barely follows cooldown. Master skills range from 15 s cooldowns (Prismatic Cascade, Kage Rush) to 120 s (Call of the Wild), but the model gave every one the same 1,000%. Across all 32 master skills (full uses counted), cooldown and damage correlate at 0.21, and 0.16 without War of Banners: close to none. Per second of cooldown, War of Banners deals 329% of a basic and Soul Vortex 20%. The median is about 70%.
  • War of Banners and Bastion of Dawn skip every cooldown cut. Their windows stamp the table's raw 60 s when they close, so they wait 60 s where every other 60 s ultimate waits 45 s. The global 0.75, the rank perk and a Quickening proc never reach them. The panel says so (v0.30.630 made it honest), but nothing else in the game works this way.
  • Spellweaver has no cap (bug B4). It is 1 s off every cooldown per critting frame, and a mage's AoE and damage-over-time kit crits several frames a second. At 40% crit it roughly tripled ultimate uptime.
  • Mind Over Matter (the maxmp + Quickening synergy) takes 0.2 s off every cooldown each regen tick, 0.4 s per second, also outside the 55% cap. Cooldowns finish in about 71% of their time, much milder than Spellweaver. Listed for completeness.
  • The rank-10 "each kill refunds 25%" perk on War of Banners mostly refunds the 250 ms re-press gate, because the real cooldown only starts when the enrage ends. It pays out only for kills in the 10 s after the enrage ends.

6. How this was measured

  • Skill table: scripts/skill_tabulation.mjs, one class per browser run, on the v0.30.1005 build. Warrior and rogue rows match the committed v0.30.814 table almost exactly. Rows driven by time (summons, orbs, damage ticks: Soul Siphon, Dark Pulse, the archer summons, Siege Volley) move 10–35% between runs with the frame rate. The only row that moved for a code reason is Meteor (bug B2).
  • Full uses: each multi-press skill mashed at the fastest rate its gate allows (never under 200 ms a press) until its real cooldown landed, then damage read for 10 s more.
  • Cooldowns: all 70 skills with a cooldown, cast at ranks 0, 5 and 10, the cooldown read after charges were spent and windows closed, compared with the Skills panel's figure. The few-percent differences it printed come from a few frames of drain before the read, not from the game.
  • Rotations: a bot presses the first ready skill in slot order (B, G, V, F, D, S, X, C), otherwise the basic, against one frozen 60 px dummy with unlimited MP, warriors tapping rather than charging. That is the ceiling cooldowns allow; real play is lower, and MP and positioning decide how much lower.
  • Spellweaver: the same rotations with real crits at 40% and real random numbers, node off vs on.
  • The cooldown fix shipped as v0.30.1016 with scripts/ult_window_repair_test.mjs (3 of 6 fail before, 6 of 6 after). The tool fix shipped as 08140288. The Skill Editor's damage test got the same fix, and the committed skill table was re-measured on v0.30.1024 with it (1be2af68), so the editor now shows War of Banners at 18,812% instead of 941%.

Not covered here: co-op (a guest's skills run the same code), crits and combo in the tables (pinned so every row is read at the same rung), and multi-target value beyond the six-monster cluster count.
