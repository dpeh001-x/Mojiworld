Mojiworld — Skill Sound Tuner

Every skill's sound effect, played exactly the way the game plays it. Listen, tell us what you think in the comment boxes, fix what sounds off with the sliders, and send it all back.

How to use this page

  1. Press ▶ Play to hear a skill's sound the way the game plays it right now.
  2. Say what you think in the 💬 comment box on its row — what's wrong, what you like, or what it should sound like instead, e.g. "too thin, should be a deep whoomph". Comments about the sounds in general go in Your comments at the bottom.
  3. Want to fix it yourself? Press ✎ Edit. Make it louder or quieter, higher or lower, skip a slow start, cut a long tail, fade it out, or make it wait a moment. ▶ Original compares it with how it was.
  4. Wrong sound altogether? Pick a different one inside the editor, or press 👎 (needs a new sound). Sounds right as it is? Press 👍.
  5. When you're done, press 💬 Export comments to send your comments, or Copy everything at the very bottom to send your slider changes as well. Paste it into a message and send it. Your work is saved in this browser as you go.

Keyboard: Space play · O original · C comment · Esc leave the comment box · E open / close the editor · 1 sounds right · 3 needs a new sound · ↓ / ↑ move · / search
